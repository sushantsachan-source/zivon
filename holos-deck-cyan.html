Why it beats Glean

Glean is the library. Aura is the operator.

Glean

Knowledge substrate
  • Searches, answers, links you out to the tool
  • Admin-defined connectors & actions
  • Agents capped (~30 min, no real runtime)
  • Horizontal — bound to each vendor's stack

Aura

Company operating layer
  • An operating graph: who owns what, what changed
  • Answer → approved action, by default
  • Real runtime — spawns sub-agents, opens PRs
  • Vendor-neutral & India-aware (voice/Hinglish)
Honest take: Glean can even be the knowledge substrate beneath Aura. The moat is execution, not search.

How we win

Buy the substrate. Build the execution.

Don't re-fight search parity. Let a commodity layer handle connectors & retrieval; spend our effort on the graph, approvals, runtime and a beautiful mobile surface.
  • Below: commodity context (Onyx-style retrieval & connectors)
  • Above: Zivon = the operating graph + approvals + runtime
  • First release: 4–5 sharp answer→action loops — ownership lookup · policy diffs · onboarding autopilot · plan-my-day · one "do-the-work" flow
